Privacy Policy for RVS Meet

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

RVS Meet ("we," "our," or "us") operates the RVS Meet video conferencing application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use RVS Meet.

By using RVS Meet, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use RVS Meet, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

Name or display name
Email address
Phone number, if provided
Profile picture, if provided
Account credentials or authentication information
Meeting Information

When you participate in a meeting, we may process:

Meeting ID
Meeting name
Participant name or display name
Meeting start and end times
Participant and host information
Meeting settings and preferences
Camera and Microphone

RVS Meet may request access to your device's camera and microphone so that you can participate in video and audio meetings.

Camera and microphone access is used to provide video and audio communication. We do not access your camera or microphone when you are not actively using a meeting feature that requires it.

Device and Technical Information

We may automatically receive limited technical information, such as:

Device type and model
Operating system and version
App version
IP address
Network information
Crash and diagnostic information
Connection quality and performance information
2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

Provide and operate RVS Meet
Create and manage user accounts
Enable video and audio conferencing
Connect meeting participants
Maintain meeting security
Improve application performance
Detect and prevent abuse or unauthorized access
Diagnose crashes and technical problems
Respond to support requests
Comply with applicable legal requirements
3. Video and Audio Communications

RVS Meet processes audio and video data to provide real-time communication between meeting participants.

Unless specifically stated within the App and enabled by the meeting host or participants, RVS Meet does not automatically record or permanently store meeting audio or video.

If recording functionality is introduced, users will be informed through the App and the applicable recording and storage practices will be disclosed before or when recording begins.
