# Privacy Policy for MarketGain

**Last Updated:** September 19, 2026

Welcome to **MarketGain** (Package Name: `com.rvs.marketgain`), developed by **RVS** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide
- **Account Details:** When you create an account or sign in, we collect your name and email address.
- **Preferences & Custom Data:** We store your custom watchlists, stock price alert triggers, algorithmic prediction preferences, and custom live market API endpoint configurations.

### B. Device & Technical Information
- **Network State & Internet Access:** To retrieve live stock market quotes (NSE/BSE), indices, and historical financial charts.
- **Notifications & Haptics:** If enabled, device notification permissions (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) and vibration permissions (`VIBRATE`) are used solely to alert you when your target price or technical breakout triggers are met.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Deliver real-time Indian stock market quotes, charts, and technical analytics.
- Save and synchronize your watchlist and user preferences.
- Dispatch local price alerts and threshold notifications when triggered.
- Improve application performance and stability.

---

## 3. Data Storage, Security & Retention

- **Local & Encrypted Storage:** Your account credentials, watchlists, and price alerts are securely stored on your device using encrypted local databases.
- **HTTPS/TLS Encryption:** All network communications between the app and live market endpoints are encrypted in transit using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols.
- **Data Retention & Deletion:** You have full control over your data. You can delete your watchlist items, clear your alert history, or delete your account at any time directly within the **Profile** section of the app.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Market Gateways

MarketGain connects to market data providers and exchange gateways to retrieve financial information:
- **Public & Licensed Market APIs:** Used strictly for fetching ticker quotes, OHLC historical date-wise bars, and sector indices.
- **Custom User Endpoints:** If you configure your own custom broker API or proxy endpoint in Settings, requests are sent directly to your specified server with the authentication headers you provide. We do not transmit your private API keys or tokens to any external third party.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

MarketGain is intended for general audiences aged 18 and older interested in financial markets and stock analytics. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 6. Financial Disclaimer

MarketGain is an educational and analytical tool providing technical indicators, historical chart patterns, and market price tracking. **The app does not provide SEBI-registered investment advice, stock brokerage services, or portfolio management.** All investments and trades are subject to market risks.

---

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
